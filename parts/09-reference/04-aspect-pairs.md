# Common aspect pairs

Taught in [Aspect: the big idea](#s-04-01) and the two sections after it; prefixes as a system are in [Verbal prefixes beyond motion](#s-05-06), and pairs of motion verbs in [Prefixed verbs of motion](#s-05-03).

Every pair is written imperfective / perfective. The pairs are grouped by how the perfective is made, because there is no way to predict which prefix a given verb takes: the groups are for recognition, the individual pairs are for learning. For conjugation oddities see [Verb conjugation tables](#s-09-03).

:::note роди́ться
In everyday use the pair is рожда́ться / роди́ться (be born), and the past tense роди́лся, родила́сь is perfective. Dictionaries still list роди́ться itself as serving both aspects, which matters only in bookish present-tense uses. The past has two accepted stresses in the masculine and plural: роди́лся or родился́, роди́лись or родили́сь; the feminine is родила́сь (роди́лась is also common in speech, as noted in [Reflexive verbs](#s-04-06)).
:::
