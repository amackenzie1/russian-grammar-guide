# Particles I: же, ведь, -то, вот, ну

Russian is full of tiny words that add no facts to a sentence, only its tone of voice. Compare:

:::ex
Я говори́л. | I said so. | a flat report
Я **же** говори́л! | I told you! | you knew this, and you ignored it
Я **ведь** говори́л. | I did say so, you know. | a reminder, offered as an explanation
**Ну** я говори́л. | Well, I said so. | shrugging: so what do you want from me?
Я**-то** говори́л. | *I* said so. | whatever anyone else did
**Вот** ви́дишь, я говори́л! | See, I said so! | pointing at the proof
:::

These words are called **particles**. English does the same work mostly with intonation, sentence stress, and phrases like "you know", "after all", "come on", "well" and "see?".

Particles carry attitude, not meaning, so they have no stable translation, and dictionary entries for them are useless ("же: and, but, as for, after all, then, very"). Ask one question about each: **what is the speaker doing with it?**

**Же** and **-то** are never stressed and lean on the word before them, so they cannot start a sentence. **Вот** and **ну** usually stand at the front. **Ведь** goes either at the front or after the first word.

## Же: "you know this"

**Же appeals to something the listener already knows, or ought to know.** It turns a statement into a reminder, usually with an edge of "obviously" or "how could you forget".

:::ex
Ты **же** зна́ешь, что я не ем мя́со. | You know I don't eat meat. | so why are you offering it?
Он **же** ребёнок. | He's a child, after all. | so what did you expect?
Мы **же** договори́лись! | But we agreed! |
Сего́дня **же** воскресе́нье, всё закры́то. | It's Sunday, remember, everything's closed. |
Я **же** не зна́ла! | Well, I didn't know! | defending yourself: surely that's obvious
:::

Же comes right after the word or phrase that carries the point, most often the first one in the sentence. In fast speech it shrinks to **ж**: «Я ж говори́л».

It is a pushy word. Among friends it is normal; said to a customer or your boss, «Я же вам объясня́л» ("I explained this to you, didn't I") sounds like the accusation it is.

### Же in questions and commands

After a question word, же adds pressure. The speaker has been wondering for a while, or is running out of patience. English uses "on earth", "ever", or just a heavier voice.

:::ex
Где **же** он? | Where on earth is he? |
Что **же** де́лать? | What are we supposed to do? |
Почему́ **же** ты молча́л? | Then why didn't you say anything? |
Кто **же** так де́лает? | Who does it like that? | meaning: nobody sane
:::

After an imperative, же is insistence: I've asked already, or it's high time.

:::ex
Иди́ **же** сюда́! | Come here, will you! |
Да скажи́ **же** [[ns:что́-нибудь]]! | Say something, for heaven's sake! |
Пойми́ **же** наконе́ц! | Will you finally understand! |
:::

This use sounds slightly dramatic and bookish. Everyday speech more often uses ну and уже́: «Ну иди́ уже́!»

### Же for "the same"

One use of же is plain grammar, not attitude. After a pointing word, же means *that very one*, and the pair translates as "the same":

| Without же | With же |
|---|---|
| тот (that) | **тот же** (the same) |
| тако́й (such) | **тако́й же** (the same kind) |
| так (so, like that) | **так же** (in the same way) |
| там (there) | **там же** (in the same place) |
| туда́ (to there) | **туда́ же** (to the same place) |
| тогда́ (then) | **тогда́ же** (at that same time) |
| сто́лько (so many) | **сто́лько же** (just as many) |

:::ex
У меня́ **тако́й же** телефо́н. | I have the same phone. | the same model, not literally yours
Мы у́чимся в **той же** шко́ле. | We go to the same school. |
Встре́тимся **там же**, где вчера́. | Let's meet in the same place as yesterday. |
Он сказа́л **то же са́мое**. | He said the very same thing. | са́мый often joins in
Я уста́л **так же**, как и ты. | I'm just as tired as you are. |
:::

With time words the same "that very" idea gives "right away, no later":

:::ex
Иди́ сюда́ **сейча́с же**! | Come here this instant! |
Я отве́чу **сего́дня же**. | I'll answer this very day. |
Он **сра́зу же** перезвони́л. | He called back right away. |
:::

:::note Так же and та́кже
Written as two words, так же means "in the same way". Written as one, та́кже means "also". The everyday word то́же ("too") is historically nothing but то же, "that same thing".
:::

## Ведь: "remember why"

**Ведь hands the listener a fact as the reason behind what was just said.** It is "after all", "you see", "you know". The fact is usually not news; the speaker is reminding, not informing.

:::ex
Не серди́сь на него́, он **ведь** хоте́л помо́чь. | Don't be angry with him; he was trying to help, after all. |
Возьми́ зонт. **Ведь** обеща́ли дождь. | Take an umbrella. They did forecast rain, you know. |
Я не могу́ ему́ позвони́ть, у меня́ **ведь** нет его́ но́мера. | I can't call him; I don't have his number, remember. |
**Ведь** я тебя́ предупрежда́л. | I did warn you, you know. |
:::

In a question shaped like a statement, ведь fishes for confirmation, like an English tag question:

:::ex
Ты **ведь** придёшь? | You are coming, aren't you? |
Э́то **ведь** не опа́сно? | This isn't dangerous, is it? |
:::

And with **а** in front, ведь marks a thought that has just dawned on the speaker, often against expectation:

:::ex
А **ведь** он прав. | You know, he's actually right. |
А **ведь** я могла́ опозда́ть! | And to think I could have been late! |
:::

### Ведь or же?

In reminders the two overlap: «Я же говори́л» and «Я ведь говори́л» describe the same event. The difference is temperature. Же presses on the listener: *you* know this, so why are you acting as if you don't? Ведь is cooler: it links a fact to a conclusion and invites the listener to agree. Же argues. Ведь explains.

Only же does the other jobs: «Где же он?», «Иди́ же!», тот же. Only ведь can open a sentence. They also combine: «Ведь я же говори́л!»

## -то: "as for this one"

You already know **-то** glued to question words to make indefinites: кто-то (someone), где-то (somewhere). The particle here is a different thing that looks the same. It attaches, with a hyphen and no stress, to any word.

**The particle -то marks its word as the topic and sets it against everything else.** "As for X..." or "X, at least...". There is always an unspoken "unlike some".

:::ex
Я**-то** зна́ю. | *I* know. | others may not
Ты**-то** отку́да зна́ешь? | And how would *you* know? |
Де́ньги**-то** есть, а вре́мени нет. | Money we've got; it's time we don't have. |
Тебе́**-то** что? | What's it to *you*? |
Сейча́с**-то** я понима́ю, а тогда́ не понима́л. | *Now* I understand, but I didn't then. |
:::

It brings up something both of you have in mind, to check on it:

:::ex
Ключи́**-то** ты взял? | The keys: did you take them? |
А Ма́ша**-то** придёт? | And what about Masha, is she coming? |
У́жинать**-то** бу́дем? | So are we having dinner or what? |
:::

Doubling a verb around -то concedes one thing before taking it back with но:

:::ex
Чита́ть**-то** я чита́л, но ничего́ не по́мню. | Oh, I read it all right, but I don't remember a thing. |
Позвони́ть**-то** он позвони́л, но так и не прие́хал. | He did call, sure, but he never came. |
:::

With не and a word of degree, -то tones a claim down to "not all that":

:::ex
Э́то не **так-то** про́сто. | It's not all that simple. |
Не **о́чень-то** и хоте́лось. | I didn't want it that much anyway. | the standard sour-grapes line
:::

And it sits inside a few fixed expressions:

:::ex
**Наконе́ц-то**! | At last! |
В **том-то** и де́ло. | That's just the point. |
**То-то** и оно́. | That's exactly it. |
Хо́лодно**-то** как! | Wow, is it cold! |
:::

## Вот: pointing

**Вот points.** With a finger, at a thing; or with the voice, at a word, a reason, a moment.

The physical use:

:::ex
**Вот** мой дом. | Here's my house. |
**Вот**, возьми́. | Here, take it. |
**Вот** тебе́ ключи́. | Here are the keys for you. |
Да́йте **вот э́тот**, пожа́луйста. | Give me this one here, please. | pointing in a shop
:::

Вот points at what is close by. For something at a distance there is a twin, **вон** ("over there"): «Вон он!» (There he is, over there!), «Вон тот дом» (that building over there), «Остано́вка вон там» (The stop is over there).

Everything else is the same gesture aimed at speech. Before a question word, вот makes that word stop asking and start pointing: "*this* is what / why / where".

:::ex
**Вот что** я ду́маю. | Here's what I think. |
**Вот почему́** я не пришёл. | That's why I didn't come. |
**Вот в чём** пробле́ма. | That's where the problem is. |
**Вот где** ты пря́чешься! | So this is where you're hiding! |
**Вот как**? | Is that so? | mild surprise
**Вот так**. | Like this. | showing how
:::

**Вот и** announces that something expected has arrived, or draws a line under what was said:

:::ex
А **вот и** Ма́ша! | And here's Masha! |
**Вот и** ле́то прошло́. | And so summer's over. |
**Вот и** всё. | And that's that. |
**Вот и** хорошо́. | Good, that's settled then. |
**Вот ви́дишь**! | There, you see! |
:::

As a reply, вот points at the other person's words and says "that, precisely":

:::ex
**Вот и́менно**! | Exactly! |
**Вот-вот**, и я о том же. | Yes, yes, that's just what I mean. |
:::

In exclamations, вот points at something remarkable. This is the Russian "what a...!" and "now that's...!":

:::ex
**Вот э́то да**! | Wow! |
**Вот э́то** но́вость! | Now that's news! |
**Вот так** сюрпри́з! | What a surprise! |
**Вот** дура́к! | What an idiot! | often about yourself
**Вот** чёрт! | Damn! |
:::

**А вот** shifts the pointer to something that contrasts with what came before:

:::ex
Ры́бу я не люблю́, **а вот** мя́со ем с удово́льствием. | I don't like fish, but meat, now, I'm happy to eat. |
**А вот** э́то уже́ интере́сно. | Now *that* is interesting. |
:::

**Вот бы** points at something that isn't there and wishes it were. It takes a past-tense form or an infinitive, like any бы:

:::ex
**Вот бы** сейча́с на мо́ре! | If only we were at the seaside right now! |
**Вот бы** он позвони́л. | I wish he'd call. |
**Вот бы** вы́спаться! | Oh, to get a proper night's sleep! |
:::

:::note Вот-вот
Doubled, вот-вот has a second meaning: "any moment now". «Он вот-вот придёт» means he'll be here any second. «Телефо́н вот-вот ся́дет» means the phone is about to die.
:::

## Ну: nudging

**Ну is the sound of a speaker reacting to the situation and nudging it along.** It is one of the most frequent particles in spoken Russian and the least translatable. "Well", "so", "come on", "oh" and a sigh all cover part of it.

**Prodding.** Ну asks the other person to get on with it:

:::ex
**Ну**? | Well? |
**Ну**, расска́зывай! | Come on, tell me! |
**Ну** пожа́луйста! | Oh, please! |
**Ну** что ты молчи́шь? | Well, why aren't you saying anything? |
**Ну** ма-ам! | Mo-om, come on! |
:::

**Asking how things stand.**

:::ex
**Ну как**? | So, how was it? How's it going? |
**Ну что**? | So? What's the verdict? |
**Ну что**, пошли́? | So, shall we go? |
:::

**Buying time.** At the start of an answer, ну says "I'm thinking":

:::ex
**Ну**, я не зна́ю… | Well, I don't know... |
**Ну**, как тебе́ сказа́ть… | Well, how can I put it... |
**Ну** не зна́ю. | Hmm, I'm not so sure. | polite disagreement
:::

**Giving in.** With a word of agreement, ну makes it reluctant, or at least considered:

:::ex
**Ну ла́дно**. | Oh, all right. |
**Ну хорошо́**, убеди́л. | Fine, you've convinced me. |
**Ну да**. | Well, yes. Yeah. | also sarcastic: "yeah, right"
**Ну дава́й**. | OK, go on then. | also a casual goodbye
**Ну коне́чно**! | But of course! | sincere or bitterly ironic, by tone
**Ну нет**. | Oh no. No way. |
:::

**Not caring.** Ну и plus a short reply is a shrug:

:::ex
**Ну и что**? | So what? |
**Ну и ла́дно**. | Fine, then. See if I care. |
**Ну и пусть**. | Let them. So be it. |
:::

**Being impressed, or appalled.** Ну и plus a noun is an exclamation about how extreme something is. Tone says which way:

:::ex
**Ну и** пого́да! | What weather! |
**Ну и** денёк! | What a day! |
**Ну и** це́ны у вас! | The prices you have here! |
**Ну и ну**! | Well, well! |
:::

**The result is in.** Ну вот greets an outcome, usually one the speaker saw coming and didn't want:

:::ex
**Ну вот**, опя́ть дождь. | There, it's raining again. |
**Ну вот**, я же говори́л. | There you go, I told you. |
**Ну вот** и всё, гото́во. | And there we are, done. | this time with satisfaction
:::

**Losing patience.**

:::ex
**Ну** ско́лько мо́жно! | How much longer! |
**Ну** почему́ всегда́ я? | Why is it always me? |
**Ну** что тако́е! | Oh, for goodness' sake! |
:::

**Not believing it.** Here ну comes second, after да:

:::ex
**Да ну**! | No way! Get out of here! |
**Да ну** тебя́! | Oh, come off it! | friendly dismissal
**Да ну** его́, э́тот фильм. | Forget that movie. | I can't be bothered with it
:::

You'll find ну again among the [fillers](#s-07-07), where it simply holds the floor while the speaker thinks.

## Hearing them together

Particles rarely travel alone. Here are О́ля and Ми́ша, late for the theater.

:::dialogue
О́ля: Ну что, ты гото́в? Мы же опа́здываем! | So, are you ready? We're late, you realize!
Ми́ша: Сейча́с, сейча́с. Где же мои́ ключи́… | Coming, coming. Where on earth are my keys...
О́ля: Вот они́, на столе́. Ты ведь сам их туда́ положи́л. | Here they are, on the table. You put them there yourself, remember.
Ми́ша: А, то́чно. Ну всё, пошли́. | Oh, right. OK, that's it, let's go.
О́ля: А биле́ты-то ты взял? | And the tickets, did you take them?
Ми́ша: Биле́ты-то я взял. Вот телефо́н найти́ не могу́. | The tickets I've got. It's my phone I can't find.
О́ля: Ну вот. Я же говори́ла: собери́ всё с ве́чера! | There you go. I told you: get everything together the night before!
Ми́ша: Ну ла́дно тебе́. Вот он, в ку́ртке. | Oh, come on. Here it is, in my jacket.
:::

Leave the particles out and every fact survives, but the couple turn into two people reading out a checklist. The English barely needed words for them: most went into italics, word order and "remember".

:::summary
- **же**: you know this (reminder, reproach); pressure in questions and commands; "the same" after тот, тако́й, там, так.
- **ведь**: here's the reason, remember; tag questions (Ты ведь придёшь?); А ведь... for a dawning thought.
- **-то**: as for this one, unlike others; verb-то... но to concede; не так-то "not all that".
- **вот**: pointing at things, words, reasons and surprises; вот и for arrivals and conclusions; вот бы for wishes.
- **ну**: nudging, stalling, giving in, shrugging, exclaiming.
:::
